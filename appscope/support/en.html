About AppScope

AppScope is a native personal study planner. Today holds your tasks, with a subject, optional due date and planned minutes. Focus offers 15, 25, 45 and 60-minute sessions with pause and resume; sessions you choose to save contribute actual focused time to a seven-day history. Library holds your searchable notes and four original offline study methods. Methods lead into your own notes so you can practise what you read.

Russian and English, light and dark appearance and Dynamic Type are supported. The core workspace works offline without an account.

Privacy Policy

Updated November 26, 2026. This policy describes the native study workspace in AppScope 1.0 (build 4). Publisher: Rachida Mbarek.

Your study records

Tasks, saved focus sessions, personal notes, timer state and preferences are stored locally on your device. AppScope does not upload these records to a publisher server. It has no user accounts, AppScope cloud service, advertising or analytics. The workspace makes no automatic network requests. System device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Your controls

You can edit and delete your own tasks and notes, mark tasks complete and choose whether to save the actual time from a focus session. Cancelling a session does not add it to saved history. Deleting the app removes its on-device data; copies in device backups remain subject to your system backup settings. There is no publisher account to delete.

Terms of Use

AppScope helps you organise personal study tasks, focus sessions and notes. Its original study methods are practical suggestions, not a promise of academic results. Planned minutes express your intention; saved focus history records the actual time you choose to save. A timer’s state restores when you return to the app; iOS controls what runs while apps are closed.

You control the study content you enter. Keep your own backup of important information using the device options available to you. AppScope is an independent project and is not affiliated with Apple Inc. No account, advertising or purchase is required to use this version.
