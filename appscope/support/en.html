About AppScope

AppScope helps you choose study and productivity tools. Four original guides suggest practical trials, comparisons show saved metadata, and personal worksheets record your experience. Search covers Apple’s Productivity and Education categories in the Russian storefront. AppScope does not certify third-party capabilities or choose a winner based on ratings. Installation takes place in the official App Store.

Privacy Policy

Updated November 25, 2026. This policy describes AppScope 1.0 with advertising disabled. Publisher: Rachida Mbarek.

Your local data

No AppScope account is required. Favorites, personal notes and checklists, search history and preferences are stored on your device. AppScope does not upload these records to a publisher server or operate app analytics. Search text is sent to Apple when you perform a search; it is not sent as analytics.

Search and images

Search and app-detail requests send your query or the requested app IDs to Apple’s iTunes Search API. Apple and image providers receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, when you request their content. Opening an App Store page uses Apple’s services. Their handling of information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy and the relevant image provider’s policy.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled in this release. The app includes Yandex advertising libraries, but does not activate Yandex/AppMetrica, request ads or download remote ad settings. There is no advertising opt-in to enable in this version. Introducing ads would require an updated app, an updated policy and the necessary consent and system permission. The Yandex Privacy Policy is provided for information about the vendor; it does not mean advertising is active here.

Control and deletion

Clear search history and the network cache in Settings. Remove favorites individually. Edit or clear notes and checklists in each app’s worksheet; removing a favorite does not delete its worksheet. Deleting AppScope removes its on-device data. Any device backups and information already processed by Apple or other providers remain subject to their services and policies.

Support and this website

If you email support, your address and the message you choose to send are used to answer your request. Do not send passwords or verification codes. Contact admin@liyanagepvtltd.com for privacy questions or to request correction or deletion of support correspondence.

This support website uses no publisher-added analytics, advertising scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosting receives ordinary web-request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Terms of Use

AppScope provides a method for independently evaluating study and productivity tools. Guides do not guarantee results; personal worksheets reflect your observations. App metadata may be outdated or incomplete. Verify download prices, subscriptions, in-app purchases, privacy, availability and terms with the developer and in the App Store. A download price does not disclose every ongoing cost.

AppScope does not sell or install third-party apps. Third-party names, descriptions, icons and images belong to their respective owners. AppScope is independent of Apple Inc.; no Apple endorsement is implied. Core features do not require an account or viewing advertising.
